A Guide to Billboard Dimensions and Formats

When planning an outdoor advertising campaign, understanding billboard dimensions in Canada is essential for reaching your target audience effectively. From large-format highway billboards that demand attention in high-speed zones to compact, street-level formats ideal for dense urban settings, choosing the right size directly impacts visibility, engagement, and return on investment.
Billboards in major Canadian markets like Vancouver, Toronto, Calgary, Montreal, Edmonton, and Ottawa come in a range of sizes designed to suit various traffic patterns, audience behaviours, and campaign goals. Whether you’re targeting daily commuters on expressways or pedestrians in bustling downtown cores, the layout and scale of your billboard play a key role in how your message is seen and remembered.
Understanding billboard dimensions goes beyond just width and height, it means evaluating viewing distance, creative space, and placement strategy. Some formats are best for broad regional exposure, while others thrive on repeated impressions in a tightly defined neighborhood. Below is a breakdown of the most common Canadian billboard sizes and how each format can be used to maximize your campaign’s impact.

Superboards (Large Format Billboards)

Typical size: 10′ × 32′ or 14′ × 48′

Superboards are the largest format of Canadian outdoor advertising. Designed for high-traffic locations such as major highways and arterial roads, these oversized formats offer exceptional visibility and extended viewing time. Superboards are ideal for brand awareness, large creative designs, and long-term campaigns that demand attention.

Horizontal Posters

Typical size: 10′ × 20′

Sometimes referred to as standard posters, these horizontal billboards are often found along commuter routes and secondary roads. They provide a strong balance between scale and budget, making them a go-to choice for businesses looking for wide local reach. Whether you’re promoting a sale, a new service area, or a seasonal special, 10x20s deliver consistent impressions at street level.

Digital Horizontal Posters

Typical size: 10′ × 20′ (Digital Rotation)

Digital poster boards follow the standard billboard dimensions of approximately 10′ × 20′, but elevate the format with vibrant, high-resolution LED screens. These digital billboards rotate multiple advertisers throughout the day and are typically located at busy intersections, near shopping centres, and in high-density traffic zones. Their dynamic nature allows for frequent creative updates, making them ideal for time-sensitive campaigns, seasonal promotions, or high-frequency brand messaging in fast-paced urban environments.

Transit Shelter Ads (TSAs)

Typical size: 47″ × 68″ (approx. 4′ × 6′)

TSAs are strategically placed at eye-level in high pedestrian traffic areas—making them extremely effective for local targeting. Whether it’s a retail location, upcoming event, or community message, transit shelters let you speak directly to foot traffic and waiting commuters across Canadian cities.
