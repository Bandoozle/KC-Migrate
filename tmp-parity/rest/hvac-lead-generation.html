HVAC Marketing

HVAC lead generation is the backbone of business growth for heating and cooling companies.

At Kosick Communications, we’ve spent decades perfecting multi-channel lead generation strategies tailored specifically to HVAC businesses across Canada and the U.S. We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all campaigns. Instead, we craft custom plans built on the foundation of real media performance and market understanding—leveraging a mix of digital marketing, TV, outdoor advertising, radio, streaming platforms, and localized opportunities to spark results.

HVAC businesses serve essential needs, heating in the winter, cooling in the summer, and energy-efficient upgrades year-round. But no matter how strong your service offering is, consistent lead generation is what fuels your calendar and keeps your technicians in the field.

Lead generation in this space is about getting in front of the right audience, homeowners, builders, property managers, at the right time, with a message that builds confidence and urgency.

Digital Advertising

Search engine marketing (Google Ads), social media campaigns (Facebook, Instagram), display ads, and geo-targeted landing pages help HVAC companies capture leads right when prospects are searching for solutions. For many of our HVAC clients, this is where the conversion funnel begins—especially for seasonal campaigns linked to rebate programs or urgent service calls.

Digital Marketing

TV & Streaming

Television remains one of the most trusted mediums in Canada and the U.S. Whether it’s 15-second spots during the evening news or pre-roll ads across streaming platforms like YouTube and CTV, we help HVAC companies build brand authority and tap into wide audiences at scale. These campaigns are especially effective when launching a new product or positioning your brand as a regional expert.

TV & Streaming

Outdoor Advertising

Billboards, superboards, and transit shelters deliver high-visibility touchpoints in strategic locations—near highways, commuter corridors, or neighbourhoods with high HVAC demand. Outdoor campaigns reinforce your brand’s physical presence and often lead to an uptick in website traffic and direct calls.

Outdoor Advertising

Radio & Audio Streaming

From traditional AM/FM radio to Spotify and podcast pre-rolls, audio advertising captures attention during drive-time, work hours, and smart-speaker listening sessions. HVAC brands benefit from frequency and message retention—especially during weather-driven campaigns like heatwave alerts or furnace maintenance promotions.

Radio Advertising

Local Opportunities

Every market is unique, and we specialize in identifying those hard-to-find opportunities that connect HVAC businesses with their communities, like sponsoring weather updates, community boards, or local news tie-ins. These grassroots efforts build familiarity and trust.
