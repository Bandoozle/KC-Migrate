Frequently Asked Questions

Yes! Our team updates and revitalizes brands to ensure your branding is relevant, appealing, and aligned with current market trends and customer preferences. Through discovery, we will refresh various elements of the brand, such as its visual identity, messaging, and positioning, while maintaining its core essence and values.

The timeline for rebranding can vary depending on the size and complexity of the brand and the scope of the rebranding project. Generally: Preparation & Research (1–2 weeks), Brand Identity Development (3–5 weeks), Messaging & Communication (4–8 weeks), Implementation & Rollout (6–12 weeks). A full rebrand typically ranges from 3 months to over a year.

Every brand is unique and the deliverables are customized for each project. Common deliverables include: brand identity elements (logo, color palette, typography, imagery guidelines), comprehensive brand guidelines document, marketing collateral templates (business cards, letterheads, presentations, social media graphics), website and digital asset implementation, and packaging/signage guidelines where applicable.

A logo is one visual element. A brand is the full impression you create, your visuals, messaging, tone, customer experience, and consistency across social, web, and advertising. Strong branding ensures people recognize you quickly and trust you faster.

We start with a short discovery process: what you sell, who you serve, what you’re best at, what competitors are doing, and what buyers actually care about. From there, we craft a clear positioning angle and messaging pillars you can use across social, web, and ads.
