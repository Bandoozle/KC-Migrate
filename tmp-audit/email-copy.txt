Email Marketing Questions
Why do you recommend Mailchimp?
−
Mailchimp offers the best combination of ease-of-use, powerful automation features, and scalability for growing businesses. Its robust segmentation, A/B testing, and analytics tools make it ideal for data-driven email marketing. We’re certified Mailchimp partners with deep expertise on the platform.
What kind of automations can you set up?
+
We build everything from simple welcome sequences to complex multi-step workflows—drip campaigns, abandoned cart recovery, re-engagement series, post-purchase follow-ups, birthday emails, and event-triggered communications. Each automation is customized to your customer journey.
How do you improve email open rates?
+
We optimize subject lines through A/B testing, segment your audience for relevance, clean your list regularly to improve deliverability, and send at optimal times based on your audience’s behavior. Most clients see open rates double within the first 90 days.
Can you migrate our existing email lists?
+
Absolutely. We handle full migrations from any platform—Constant Contact, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, or custom systems. We’ll clean your data, map your fields, preserve segmentation, and set up proper tagging so nothing is lost in the transition.
How often should we be sending emails?
+
It depends on your industry and audience. Most businesses benefit from 1-2 campaigns per week plus automated sequences. We’ll analyze your engagement data and find the frequency that maximizes opens and clicks without causing list fatigue.
