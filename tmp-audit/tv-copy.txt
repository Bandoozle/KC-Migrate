Frequently Asked Questions
How much does TV advertising cost?
−
TV advertising costs vary widely based on market, network, daypart, and campaign duration. Local spots can start from a few hundred dollars, while national campaigns require larger budgets. We work with your budget to maximize reach and frequency, negotiating the best possible rates through our network relationships.
What’s the difference between linear TV and connected TV?
+
Linear TV is traditional television advertising delivered through broadcast television and viewed on a set schedule through cable or antenna. Connected TV (CTV) refers to streaming content on internet connected devices like Smart TVs, Roku, and Apple TV. CTV offers advanced audience targeting and measurable performance, while linear TV provides broad mass market reach and strong brand credibility.
How do you measure TV advertising effectiveness?
+
We use a combination of traditional metrics like GRPs and reach/frequency, alongside modern attribution tools including website lift studies, search volume correlation, and CTV-specific conversion tracking. Every campaign includes detailed performance reporting tied to your business objectives.
Which networks and platforms do you work with?
+
We have direct relationships with major networks including CTV, Global, CBC, Sportsnet, TSN, and Bell Media, as well as U.S. networks like NBC, CBS, FOX, CNN, and ESPN. For streaming, we work across all major CTV and OTT platforms.
Can you handle the creative production for TV Commercials?
+
Absolutely. Our in-house creative team handles everything from concept development and scriptwriting to full production and post-production. We create compelling TV commercials that resonate with your target audience and align with your brand strategy.
What’s the minimum commitment for a TV campaign?
+
We recommend a minimum 4-week flight to build sufficient frequency and brand recall. However, we tailor every campaign to your goals and budget, whether that’s a short-burst awareness push or a sustained annual media plan.
